# Cite the ML-Agents Toolkit

Reference the ML-Agents Toolkit in your research with these BibTeX citations.

For a discussion of Unity as an AI platform, refer to the [reference paper on Unity and the ML-Agents Toolkit](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.02627).

## Cite the Unity platform paper

If you use Unity or the ML-Agents Toolkit in your research, cite the following paper:

```
@article{juliani2020,
  title={Unity: A general platform for intelligent agents},
  author={Juliani, Arthur and Berges, Vincent-Pierre and Teng, Ervin and Cohen, Andrew and Harper, Jonathan and Elion, Chris and Goy, Chris and Gao, Yuan and Henry, Hunter and Mattar, Marwan and Lange, Danny},
  journal={arXiv preprint arXiv:1809.02627},
  url={https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.02627.pdf},
  year={2020}
}
```

## Cite the MA-POCA trainer

If you use the MA-POCA trainer in your research, also cite the following paper:

```
@article{cohen2022,
  title={On the Use and Misuse of Absorbing States in Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning},
  author={Cohen, Andrew and Teng, Ervin and Berges, Vincent-Pierre and Dong, Ruo-Ping and Henry, Hunter and Mattar, Marwan and Zook, Alexander and Ganguly, Sujoy},
  journal={RL in Games Workshop AAAI 2022},
  url={http://aaai-rlg.mlanctot.info/papers/AAAI22-RLG_paper_32.pdf},
  year={2022}
}
```
